# Data Privacy and Research Ethics

This project uses human-participant gait and cognitive-status data. The source files include direct identifiers and sensitive behavioral measurements. For that reason, this repository intentionally excludes:

- participant names, ages, IDs, and clinical labels;
- raw or processed gait videos;
- OpenPose keypoint trajectories and participant-level feature vectors;
- train/test assignments and row-level predictions;
- consent forms, ethics records, and internal research documents;
- the unpublished manuscript and third-party thesis materials.

Only aggregate research descriptions and selected non-identifying figures are included. Repository access does not grant permission to reconstruct, infer, or request the identity or health status of any participant.

The study materials identify institutional review approval from the Institute of Psychology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (Approval No. H15010). Any future data sharing must follow the consent terms, institutional requirements, and approval of the principal investigator and relevant data owners.
